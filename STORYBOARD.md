# The Executioner's Apprentice

A music video for Death Grips, "Guillotine (It goes Yah)". Full song, 0:00 to 3:42.

## The song

- Tempo 75.86 BPM (half-time feel), first downbeat at 0.166 s. One bar = 3.164 s. `bar(n) = 0.166 + 3.164 * n`.
- The vocals end around 218.5 s and the tail rings out to about 221 s. The video runs 222 s.
- Four **breaks** where the track drops out for one bar: bars 8, 23, 33, 48. Each break is a **blade drop**.
- Heavy low hits at bars 16 and 19, a bass hit at bar 28, a bright spike at bar 39, a hush at bars 42 to 43.
- The outro (bars 53 to 68) alternates two 4-bar patterns: **C** (bars 53-56, 61-64) and **A** (bars 57-60, 65-68).

## Plan

**Logline.** Clawd, the dark gray apprentice who sweeps under a town's giant guillotine, wants to protect one small red flower, but the Headsman chops everything Clawd loves, so Clawd brings the whole guillotine down and the town blooms.

**World.** One town square with cobbles, a wooden scaffold, the guillotine in the centre, crooked bone-coloured houses and a hut for the Headsman.
Palette: bone paper (`#EFE6D2`), charcoal ink, dark gray Clawd and one blood red (`#B3261E`) that starts as a single flower and takes over the frame by the end.

**Colour arc.** Pale foggy dawn (only the flower is red) → harsh noon (red melons and the Headsman's hood) → blue-gray rain (grief) → charcoal night with a moon and a lightning flash (the plan) → a red dawn creeping up (the showdown) → a hot red sunrise (the fall and the dance) → a warm bone morning covered in red flowers.

**Motif.** The red flower. Clawd waters it in the first shot, the Headsman chops it at break 2, Clawd keeps one petal, plants it where the guillotine stood, and it blooms into a square full of flowers. Second motif: **the blade drop**, one per break, each hitting something closer to Clawd, until the fourth one takes down the guillotine itself.

**Cast.**
- **Clawd.** Dark gray body (`#4B4B52`, dark `#34343A`, light `#6E6E76`). Eyes and marks are painted bone so they read on the gray. Same shape as the default Clawd.
- **The Headsman.** About 2.5x Clawd's height, charcoal body, an oxblood hood with two eye holes that glow faint red when angry, huge hands. He stomps on the beat. Under the hood (revealed at the end): a small, nervous round face.
- **Townsfolk.** Five bone-coloured bean-shaped people with dot eyes, each with its own hat (bonnet, flat cap, top hat, headscarf, bald with a moustache) and its own bounce timing. They cheer at every drop until the end, when they switch sides.
- **Props.** The guillotine (charcoal wood, bone-silver blade, a fraying rope, a red lever), red melons (the "executions": a cartoon red splash, never anything alive), Clawd's broom, a watering can, the red flower.

**Clawd's arc.** happy (tending the flower) → nervous → scared → sad and cry (the flower) → angry → determined (the climb, the lid open) → furious (the bite) → surprised → proud → excited and love (the bloom) → happy and relieved (the final shot).

**Lyrics.** This storyboard follows the song's structure and mood rather than individual lines. If the lyrics are added with timestamps, individual gestures (the Headsman's stomps, the crowd's shouts, Clawd's glares) get re-timed to land on specific lines.

## Production notes

- **Draft mode.** A `?draft` flag swaps the watercolour `fill` for flat `wash` so review sheets render quickly without a GPU. The final render uses the full watercolour look.
- **Build order.** Section by section (intro + S1 first), reviewing each with sheets and strips before moving on. Each section is pushed as it's finished.
- **Final render** on a GPU machine:
  ```bash
  npm install
  node render.mjs --frames --workers=4
  node render.mjs --encode --audio=assets/guillotine.mp3 --out=out/video.mp4
  ```
